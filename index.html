SYSTEM DESIGN

Experiments should produce evidence, not just demos.

Reproducible. Workloads are described as explicit manifests and expanded into concrete run specifications.

Observable. Raw events, timings, model/runtime metadata and per-query outputs remain available after execution.

Comparable. Baselines and variants are paired wherever possible instead of reducing a system to one headline number.

Modular. Model runtimes, retrieval stages and reporting layers are separated so individual components can be replaced and measured.
