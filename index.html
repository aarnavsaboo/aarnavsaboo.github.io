ABOUT

More interested in what works than what sounds impressive.

I'm Aarnav Saboo. My interests sit between applied NLP and the engineering around language models: document processing, retrieval, structured extraction, evaluation and model integrations.

I like projects where you can inspect each step. Which passage was retrieved? What changed when the chunk size changed? Did an output actually satisfy the schema, or just look convincing?

These repositories are evolving experiments, not claims of a finished platform. The aim is to make the useful parts runnable, testable and easy to build on.
